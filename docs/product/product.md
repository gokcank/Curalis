# Product Vision

## Purpose

This document defines what Curalis is, who it is for, and what problems it aims to solve.

It serves as the product foundation for all future design, engineering, and architectural decisions.

When implementation decisions conflict with the product vision, the product vision should be reconsidered before changing the product itself.

---

# Vision

Curalis is a modern medication management application that helps people organize medications, treatment schedules, reminders, and medication history in a simple, reliable, and privacy-focused way.

The application should reduce cognitive load, improve medication adherence, and make medication management effortless.

---

# Mission

Help users take the right medication at the right time while keeping complete control over their personal medical information.

---

# Core Values

## Privacy First

Users own their medical data.

Medical information should never be collected, analyzed, or shared without explicit user consent.

---

## Reliability

Medication reminders should be dependable.

Core functionality must continue working without internet access.

---

## Simplicity

Medication management should require as little effort as possible.

Every screen should have a clear purpose.

---

## Transparency

Users should always know:

- where information comes from
- what data is stored
- what actions are performed automatically

Nothing should happen silently.

---

## Accessibility

Medication management should be usable by everyone.

Accessibility is a requirement, not an optional feature.

---

# Target Audience

Curalis is designed for people who:

- take medications regularly
- manage chronic conditions
- take temporary treatments
- manage medications for family members
- prefer privacy-focused applications

---

# Non-Goals

Curalis is NOT intended to:

- diagnose diseases
- replace healthcare professionals
- recommend treatments
- prescribe medications
- calculate medical dosages
- interpret laboratory results

Medical decisions always belong to qualified healthcare professionals.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature should satisfy at least one of the following goals:

- reduce missed doses
- reduce medication errors
- simplify medication management
- improve organization
- improve accessibility
- improve reliability

Features that do not contribute to these goals should be reconsidered.

---

# MVP

The initial version focuses on:

- Medication management
- Medication reminders
- Treatment schedules
- Local database
- Provider integration
- Manual medication creation
- Inventory tracking
- Medication history

Cloud synchronization is intentionally excluded.

---

# Future Vision

Future versions may include:

- Cloud synchronization
- Multi-device support
- Family sharing
- Wearables
- Prescription import
- OCR
- Barcode scanning
- Medication interaction checking
- AI-assisted medication organization
- Healthcare integrations

Future features must not compromise privacy or reliability.

---

# Success Metrics

Curalis is successful if users can:

- add medications quickly
- receive reliable reminders
- access medication information offline
- manage treatments without confusion
- trust the application with sensitive information

---

# Design Philosophy

The application should feel:

- calm
- predictable
- responsive
- uncluttered
- trustworthy

Visual complexity should never interfere with medication management.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Curalis aims to become a complete personal medication management platform while remaining privacy-first and offline-capable.

Growth should never compromise the project's core principles.
