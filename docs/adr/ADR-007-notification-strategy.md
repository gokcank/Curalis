# ADR-007

# Notification Strategy

## Status

Accepted

---

## Date

2026-08-01

---

## Context

Medication reminders are one of the core responsibilities of Curalis.

Notifications must be dependable, respectful, and easy to understand.

---

## Problem

How should reminder notifications behave?

---

## Decision

Notifications should prioritize reliability over richness.

Every reminder should provide immediate access to relevant actions without overwhelming the user.

---

## Principles

Notifications should be:

- Timely
- Actionable
- Concise
- Predictable

---

## Rules

Notifications should never imply medical advice.

Snoozing, confirming, or dismissing reminders should require minimal interaction.

Notification wording must follow the Content Guidelines.

---

## Future

Future releases may support wearable devices and cross-device notifications without changing the notification philosophy.
